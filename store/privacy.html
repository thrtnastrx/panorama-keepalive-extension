Privacy Policy — Panorama Keep-Alive

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Panorama Keep-Alive is a browser extension that refreshes an existing Palo Alto Panorama or Strata Cloud Manager web login in this browser so idle timeouts are less likely to sign you out.

  • The extension does not collect account registration data.
  • It does not store Panorama or SCM passwords.
  • It uses cookies already present in this browser, plus settings you enter (hosts, aliases, duration, and refresh interval). Those settings stay in local extension storage on this device.
  • Keep-alive requests are sent only to hosts you enable. For Strata Cloud Manager, the extension may open or reuse a background tab on stratacloudmanager.paloaltonetworks.com.
  • The extension does not sell user data and does not send data to the publisher.
  • You can remove stored settings by removing the extension.
